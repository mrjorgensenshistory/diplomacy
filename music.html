Music Room — the War Room soundtrack

How it works in class

Music plays from the teacher's screen only (the War Room has a 🎵 player) — not on 28 student Chromebooks. The victory fanfare sounds automatically when any board crowns a winner. Default theme: Hero Down, because WW1 is a sad game.
