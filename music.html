Music Audition Room — pick your soundtrack

How to choose

Press play on anything. You're picking up to three roles: ① a background loop kids hear while plotting orders, ② a victory fanfare when someone wins, ③ a defeat lament for the fallen. Tell Claude your picks (e.g., "background: Covert Affair, victory: fanfare, defeat: Hero Down") and they get wired into the game. Music will be off by default with a 🎵 button — nothing autoplays at full volume across 28 Chromebooks.
